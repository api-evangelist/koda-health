For the first time, CMS has made Advance Care Planning a finalized federal hospital quality measure. The FY2027 IPPS final rule (CMS-1849-F) adopts the ACP eCQM (CMS1317) into Hospital IQR, Promoting Interoperability, and the PPS-Exempt Cancer Hospital Quality Reporting Program (PCHQR). Reporting starts CY2028.
